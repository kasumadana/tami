# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Primary:** Students & Young Teens (Ages 8–15) navigating digital spaces, social media, messaging apps, and online gaming on mobile and desktop devices. They need a non-judgmental, psychologically safe environment to inspect suspicious messages and build digital self-defense instincts.
- **Secondary:** Educators & Parents seeking structured curriculum modules, guided family discussion points, and clear threat trends to facilitate cybersecurity literacy in classrooms and households.

## Product Purpose

tami (Teman Aman Media Internet) is an AI Smart Tutor and interactive cybersecurity education web platform designed to train critical thinking and digital self-defense instincts in students through Socratic dialog guidance, interactive defense simulations, and multimodal forensic visual inspection of digital threats (phishing, social engineering, game scams, cyberbullying).

Success means students independently recognize risk indicators rather than relying on memorized rules, achieving high Socratic compliance, rapid multimodal visual threat analysis, and verified learning outcomes with digital hero certifications.

## Brand Personality

- **3-Word Personality:** Friendly, Inquisitive, Shielding
- **Mascot Persona:** Tami the Red Panda — observant, courteous, encouraging, and sharp. Speaks with warmth and clarity without sounding patronizing or overly childish.
- **Voice & Tone:** Empowering and exploratory. Asks guiding questions that ignite reasoning ("What feels unusual about this link?") rather than delivering curt verdicts.
- **Emotional Goals:** Psychological safety, curiosity, mastery, and confidence.

## Anti-references

- **Dark-Hacker & Neon-Matrix Tropes:** No green-on-black terminal fonts, skull graphics, or Hollywood-style cyberpunk scare tactics that intimidate young learners.
- **Sterile Corporate SaaS & Bureaucratic Portals:** Avoid dry, corporate enterprise compliance dashboards and rigid textbook-style government LMS interfaces.
- **Babyish & Overly Cartoonish Clutter:** Avoid infantilizing aesthetics, messy gamified glitter, and noisy distractions that treat 12-15 year olds like toddlers.
- **Generic AI Beige/Cream Monoculture:** Avoid washed-out low-contrast warm parchment backgrounds, generic AI card templates, and empty decorative glassmorphism.
- **Cryptic Cybersecurity Jargon:** No unexplained acronyms (e.g., DNS, MITM, XSS, Typosquatting) presented without plain-language metaphors.

## Design Principles

- **Socratic by Design (Guide, Don't Hand Answers):** Interfaces lead with discovery, reflective inquiry, and interactive breakdowns before giving conclusions.
- **Zero-Fear Safe Harbor:** Every screen conveys safety, approachable reassurance, and clear error recovery so children never feel blamed for encountering scams.
- **Tactile Defense & Tangible Feedback:** Interactive simulations (phishing audits, password labs, visual threat breakdowns) provide immediate, tactile feedback with clear risk states.
- **Clarity Over Clutter:** Clean, structured information hierarchy optimized for focus, high readability, and effortless comprehension across mobile and desktop.
- **Strict Bilingual Equity:** Seamless, culturally attuned support for Indonesian (`/`) and English (`/en`) with zero compromised or hardcoded strings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Target Compliance:** WCAG 2.1 AA contrast compliance across all text and interactive controls (≥4.5:1 for body text, ≥3:1 for large display elements).
- **Physical Controls:** Touch targets strictly $\ge 44 \times 44\text{px}$ for mobile and touchscreen resilience.
- **Cognitive & Motion Comfort:** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with instant transitions and zero forced distracting animations. High-contrast semantic indicators for color-blind learners (icons paired with color states).
- **Adaptive Theming:** Flawless dual support for Light Mode and Dark Mode with consistent semantic tokens.
